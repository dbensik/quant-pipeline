## The mechanism — why a hole is permanent

`core/ingest.py` starts each symbol's window at `newest_stored + 1 day`. Once
ANY later bar is stored, an earlier missing day is never requested again. A
transient provider error, a partial response, an aborted morning — anything
that loses one day while a later day lands — leaves a hole forever, silently.
Nothing detects it: the reassignment check needs a regime change, freshness
looks only at the newest bar, and `max_gap` fires at 30 days.

## Consequences

- Every return series spanning 2026-08-28 merges two days into one for 463
  names. Daily volatility, drawdowns and any day-indexed signal are off there;
  cumulative returns are unaffected (the endpoints are right).
- Cross-sectional work on 2026-08-28 itself has 64 names instead of 527.

## Proposed, not done

1. **Fill the known holes** — fetch only the missing days and INSERT; never
   `replace=True`. Additive, so it cannot destroy anything (unlike
   `--full-backfill`, see para-findings-2026-09-24.md). Adjustment is as of
   today, so a filled bar can sit ~1% off its neighbours on a dividend payer —
   the same drift every incremental bar already has.
2. **Make ingest self-healing** — detect missing trading days against a
   reference calendar and include them in the next window, or at minimum
   start the window several sessions back and insert-only (DXCM/MGM suggest a
   day can stay unavailable for two runs). Log `skipped_empty` per symbol so
   the next one can be traced.
3. **Detect it** — add missing-interior-days to the daily checks, so the next
   one is reported the morning after, not found a month later by accident.

## Done, 2026-09-25

- **Self-healing ingest.** An incremental run now reaches back
  `INGEST_OVERLAP_DAYS = 14` behind the newest stored bar and inserts only what
  is missing; held bars are never rewritten. Filled holes are counted
  (`SymbolOutcome.filled`, logged per symbol and in the CLI summary), and
  dropped empty bars are logged with their dates. A dead symbol is judged on
  bars NEWER than those held, so the overlap cannot hide a delisting.
- **Detection.** `scripts/check_missing_days.py` runs after ingest in the 06:00
  job. Calendar: stored SPY plus a fresh Yahoo SPY fetch. Exit 1 only for a
  hole older than the overlap; younger ones are PENDING.
- **Filled.** Live acceptance test first: a routine run of DXCM and MGM filled
  exactly their 2026-09-22 (89.53 and 38.90, matching Yahoo) and nothing else.
  Then `--start 2026-08-27` (new, insert-only) over the 463 symbols missing
  2026-08-28: 463 rows inserted, one per symbol; `market_data` 1,055,286 ->
  1,055,751 (+465 = 463 + DXCM + MGM). EIX now reads -4.8% then -23.1%.
- **Left:** FISV 2025-11-12 — Yahoo does not serve that day (its series goes
  11-11 to 11-13), so it cannot be filled. Needs a call: accept it via
  `metadata.missing_days_accepted`, or leave the check failing.
- Filled bars are adjusted as of 2026-09-25, their neighbours as of their own
  fetch dates. Measured against fresh Yahoo returns for all 463: median
  difference 0.0pp on both 08-28 and 08-31; 22 symbols over 1pp, worst VICI at
  1.9pp, always equal and opposite across the two days — a dividend-adjustment
  offset on the filled bar, the same drift every incremental bar has.
