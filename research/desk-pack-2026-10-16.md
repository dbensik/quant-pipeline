# A data pack for the Equity Research Desk (decisions 39, 40; 2026-10-16)

The Equity Research Desk (`/equity-desk`, spec in the vault at
`Bensik & Co/Desks/Equity Research Desk.md`) gathers its own data on every
run. Its run log records the cost: aggregator figures reaching all five
analysts (runs 1-2, nine figures, all killed by Risk), an undated price that
moved 15% before the run (run 4, step 3b), a figure losing its provenance
between seats (run 5, step 3c). The pipeline already holds most of that data,
refreshed daily from the same primary source, with provenance attached.

## 39. The pack — built

`python scripts/desk_pack.py TICKER --out <run folder> [--as-of DATE] [--trigger PRICE --by DATE [--below]]`

| file | what | seats |
|---|---|---|
| `screen.json` | sector screen for the conflict rule (step 2) | all |
| `facts.json` | every stored SEC fact filed on or before as_of: concept, period, value, form, **filed date, accession, EDGAR directory URL** | analysts, refutation |
| `statements.json` | annual and quarterly statements as filed (the Research page's source) | analysts, refutation |
| `prices.json` | dated: last close and day, returns 1m-10y, volatility, drawdowns, 52-week range, month-end closes | all |
| `valuation.json` | the DCF as of as_of, every input with its source, the first-year growth the price implies | valuation sanity, refutation |
| `valuation_history.json` | value/price at each past filing, with decision 21's caveat (it does not rank returns) | valuation sanity, refutation |
| `options.json` | ATM term structure; with `--trigger/--by`, the options-implied probability (Check 1) | valuation sanity, gate |
| `pack.json` | status, pipeline commit, every file's sha256, seat routing, what is not covered | — |

- **Point in time.** Facts filed on or before `as_of`, prices on or before it,
  the DCF and its history as of it. Integration test: a pack as of 2020-06-30
  holds no fact filed or price dated after it.
- **The analysts never see the DCF.** A shared valuation would anchor all five
  to one view; `pack.json` routes it to the valuation-sanity seat only.
- **Refused before gathering.** A Utilities name exits 3 with only
  `screen.json` written; a name outside the registry exits 2 (gather the usual
  way). The sector screen is a first pass — ETRM vendors and merchant-power
  counterparties are not a sector, so step 2's judgement still runs.
- **Provenance.** Each fact carries its accession, so step 3c's "document and
  date inline" is met by construction, and `pack.json`'s sha256s go straight
  into the run manifest's inputs table.

Measured: MSFT as of 2026-10-16 in 2.6 s — 4,811 facts across 33 concepts,
4,222 bars, DCF $139.75 against $539.44 (the price implies 84.5% first-year
growth), 67 history points. **The implied probability matches the market's
own quotes:** SPY P(below 700 by 2026-12-31) from the pack 0.067; from the
strike-slope of the raw put quotes around 700 (699 4.76, 701 4.895 mid) 0.068.
It is taken from the slope of call prices across strikes, each at its own
smile vol, not N(d2) at one vol — with equity skew those differ (P(above 777)
0.597 against a flat 0.489). Option chains are archived for SPY, QQQ, IWM,
AAPL, NVDA and TQQQ only; anything else says so.

Tests: `tests/unit/test_desk_pack.py` (13), `tests/test_desk_pack_integration.py` (3).

| a. Insider transactions | Form 4 XML via the EDGAR daily index the 06:00 job already reads | 3-4 h build, backfill ~2 years | new table; feeds Management & incentives |

| d. Executive pay | proxy DEF 14A, Pay-versus-Performance inline XBRL (`ecd`) | 4-6 h | must fetch and parse each proxy; parsing risk |
| e. Risk factors / MD&A text | 10-K primary documents, Items 1A and 7 | 4-5 h | stored text with accession; a year-over-year risk-factor diff for the Bear seat |

analysts' work, not data.

reads, each lands in the pack as one file, and together they cover the two

run on the pack and said what it lacked.
